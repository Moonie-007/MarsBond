# Marsbound 
It is a 3D survival game set on the hostile surface of Mars, where players must survive for 100 in-game days after a catastrophic spaceship crash leaves them as the only survivor.

Stranded on an unfamiliar planet, players must hunt alien creatures for food, manage their hunger, and survive increasingly dangerous nights. Every day brings another challenge, and survival is the only way home.

## Game Overview
- 3D Survival
-Third-Person / First-Person (depending on implementation)
-Godot Engine 4
-Single Player

## Story

A mission to Mars takes an unexpected turn when your spaceship crashes onto the planet's surface.

You are the only survivor.

With no immediate way to return home, you must adapt to the harsh Martian environment and survive for 100 days until you can finally make your way back.

However, Mars is not as lifeless as it seems. Hostile alien creatures roam the planet, and surviving each night becomes a fight against hunger and danger.

## 🕹️ Gameplay Features

- Stay alive for 100 in-game days.
- Encounter hostile creatures inhabiting the Martian surface.
- Hunt smaller alien creatures to obtain food.
- Manage your food supply to avoid starvation.
- Experience changing environmental conditions as day turns into night.
- Larger and more powerful aliens emerge after dark.
- Defend yourself against alien attacks.
- Each completed day brings you closer to returning home.

## Objective

Your primary objective is simple:

**Survive 100 days on Mars and make it back home.**

But reaching that goal requires careful resource management, awareness of your surroundings, and the ability to defend yourself against hostile creatures.
